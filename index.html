Happy 21st birthday, my love. ❤️

You’re 21 now.

I know I don’t always say everything I feel, but having you in my life means more to me than I probably show. There’s something about you that makes ordinary days feel a little more special, and I’m grateful for every moment we get to share.

As you step into 21, I hope you keep chasing the things you want, keep believing in yourself, and never lose the little things that make you you. And whatever this year brings, I hope I can be there beside you—to celebrate the good days, get through the difficult ones, and make a few unforgettable memories together.

Sometimes when I look at you, I realize how young you still are. You want to go out, find pretty cafés, dress up, take beautiful pictures, and turn even an ordinary day into a little memory.

And then there’s me…

The grumpy old man who complains about the taxi fare, complains that the food at the café doesn’t even fill my stomach, complains about how long we’ve been taking pictures, and somehow always has something to say.

“Do we really need to take this many pictures?”
“How long are you going to take?”
“This place is expensive.”
“You forgot your umbrella again. How careless can you be?”

That’s me.

And honestly, I know I can be annoying sometimes.

I know there have been times when you just wanted to enjoy yourself, take some pictures and have a good day, while I was standing there impatiently complaining about something. Sometimes we’ve even argued because of it.

I’m sorry for those moments.

I know that when I complain, you might feel like I don’t enjoy doing those things with you, or that I don’t care about the things that make you happy.

But the truth is a little different.

I may never be the person who gets excited about going to every café or taking hundreds of pictures. I may always calculate the taxi fare in my head and wonder why the food costs so much when it doesn't even fill me up.

But I’m slowly realizing that the point was never really the café.

It was you.

It was seeing you happy because you found a place you liked.

It was watching you get excited about how you looked in a picture.

It was holding the phone and taking one more photo even after I had already said, “Okay, that's enough.”

And maybe one day, when we're much older, those are exactly the things I'm going to miss.

Not the money I spent.
Not the time I thought I wasted.
Not the food that didn't fill me up.

But you standing in front of me, asking me to take “just one more picture.” ❤️

I know I’m not always good at showing love.

Sometimes I care too much about small things. Sometimes my mouth speaks before my heart gets the chance to explain itself. I complain, I tease you, I get impatient, and sometimes I say things that I probably should have kept to myself.

But beneath all of that, there is someone who cares about you more than he knows how to say.

And I hope you know that person is me.

I hope you enjoy being 21.

Go to the cafés you want to go to. Take all the pictures you want. Dress up. Go places. Laugh loudly. Make silly memories. Enjoy being young.

And if I’m lucky enough to be beside you through all of it, I’ll probably still complain.

I'll probably still say the taxi is too expensive.
I'll probably still complain that the food isn't enough.
I'll probably still ask, “How many more pictures?”
And I'll probably still call you careless when you forget your umbrella.

But I'll still be there.

I'll take the pictures.
I'll wait for you.
I'll go to those places with you.

And I'll secretly enjoy seeing you happy, even while pretending to be annoyed.

Because I don't just want to be there for the perfect moments.

I want to be there for the silly arguments, the random café trips, the countless photos, the forgotten umbrellas, the ordinary days, and all the little moments that we don't realize are precious until they're already memories.

I don't know what the next few years will look like.

But I hope when you look back at your 21st birthday someday, you remember that you were loved.

Deeply.

Even by that grumpy, stingy old man who was always complaining beside you. ❤️

Happy 21st birthday, baby.

I hope this year gives you countless reasons to smile, beautiful places to see, memories worth keeping, and dreams worth chasing.

And I hope I get to be in a few of those memories too.

I love you. ❤️
